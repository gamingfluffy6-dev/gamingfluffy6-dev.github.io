Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ClipKaro93 Uploader ("the app") is used only by the owner of the ClipKaro93 YouTube channel.

What data the app uses

  • An OAuth access token for the ClipKaro93 channel, granted by the channel owner, with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • Our own channel's video list and public statistics (views, likes, comments) returned by the YouTube Data API.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload our own videos to our own channel and to read our own channel's statistics. Statistics are stored in a local file on the owner's computer to compare clip performance.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to any third party. No data about other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token file also stops all access.

This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
